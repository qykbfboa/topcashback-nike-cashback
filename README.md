## When Cash Back doesn't track — and how to prevent it

This is the part nobody likes to talk about, but it's the part that actually causes problems. Sometimes a purchase tracks, and sometimes it doesn't. TopCashback's own blog lays out the most common reasons:

- **Ad blockers or cookie blockers running** during the purchase. Disable them, or pause them for the session.
- **Browser cache full of old tracking cookies**. Clear cache before you start a fresh shopping session.
- **Navigating away mid-session** — opening email, checking another tab, browsing a different site before checkout. The tracking cookie can get overwritten.
- **Starting with items already in your Nike cart**. Empty the cart first, then click through TopCashback, then add items.
- **Using a coupon code from somewhere other than TopCashback**. This is the #1 cause of declined Cash Back.
- **Returning or canceling the order**. Cash Back gets reversed on returns.
- **Buying through Nike's mobile app** instead of the website. Some merchants don't track in-app purchases — check the merchant's terms on TopCashback before assuming app purchases count.

If a transaction genuinely doesn't track and it should have, TopCashback has a missing cashback claim process. You submit your order confirmation, and they'll chase it with the retailer. It's not instant, but it works more often than not in my experience.
